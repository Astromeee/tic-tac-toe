# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University classmates taking short breaks together. They want a game that is immediately understandable, quick to start, and enjoyable on either a laptop or phone.

## Product Purpose

Arcade Break is a lightweight home for simple browser games. Tic-Tac-Toe is the first playable title; the shell should make future games easy to discover without making the current experience feel unfinished.

## Brand Personality

Playful, fun, and energetic. The voice is friendly and concise, with the confident tactility of a favorite handheld console.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon overload, illegible pixel typography, decorative glassmorphism, generic SaaS cards, and motion that delays play.

## Design Principles

- Get players into the game immediately.
- Make every action feel tactile and responsive.
- Use nostalgia as flavor, never as a barrier to readability.
- Let the shared arcade shell grow without crowding the active game.
- Celebrate wins without punishing motion-sensitive players.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard play, visible focus, 44px minimum targets, live game announcements, color-independent player marks, and reduced-motion preferences.
